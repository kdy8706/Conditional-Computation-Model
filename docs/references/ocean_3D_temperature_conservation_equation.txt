Temperature Conservation Equation in the Ocean
================================================

1. Three-dimensional temperature conservation equation
--------------------------------------------------------

The temperature field T(x, y, z, t) in the ocean can be represented by the
three-dimensional advection-diffusion equation:

∂T/∂t
+ u ∂T/∂x
+ v ∂T/∂y
+ w ∂T/∂z
=
∇_h · (K_h ∇_h T)
+ ∂/∂z (K_T ∂T/∂z)
+ Q_T


Equivalent compact form:

∂T/∂t + u⃗ · ∇T
=
∇_h · (K_h ∇_h T)
+ ∂/∂z (K_T ∂T/∂z)
+ Q_T


2. Meaning of each term
-----------------------

[Local temporal change]

∂T/∂t

- Temporal change of temperature at a fixed location.


[Horizontal advection]

u ∂T/∂x + v ∂T/∂y

- Transport of temperature by horizontal ocean currents.
- u: zonal (east-west) velocity
- v: meridional (north-south) velocity


[Vertical advection]

w ∂T/∂z

- Vertical transport of temperature by vertical velocity.
- w > 0 generally represents upward motion when z is defined positive upward.
- This term is directly related to processes such as upwelling, downwelling,
  mesoscale/submesoscale vertical motions, and overturning circulation.


[Horizontal diffusion/mixing]

∇_h · (K_h ∇_h T)

- Horizontal redistribution of temperature by unresolved turbulent mixing.
- K_h: horizontal diffusivity [m² s⁻¹]


[Vertical diffusion/mixing]

∂/∂z (K_T ∂T/∂z)

- Vertical redistribution of temperature by turbulent or molecular diffusion.
- K_T: vertical thermal diffusivity [m² s⁻¹]
- This term is especially important in the surface mixed layer, thermocline,
  and regions of strong turbulent mixing.


[Source/sink term]

Q_T

- Represents heating or cooling processes not explicitly included in the
  advection-diffusion terms.
- Depending on formulation, it may include surface heat flux, shortwave
  radiation penetration, internal heating, or parameterized source/sink terms.


3. Terms directly associated with vertical temperature transport
-----------------------------------------------------------------

The two principal terms controlling vertical temperature redistribution are:

(1) Vertical advection

w ∂T/∂z


(2) Vertical diffusion / turbulent mixing

∂/∂z (K_T ∂T/∂z)


Therefore, a simplified one-dimensional vertical temperature equation can be
written as:

∂T/∂t
+ w ∂T/∂z
=
∂/∂z (K_T ∂T/∂z)
+ Q_T


4. Vertical heat flux formulation
---------------------------------

Temperature transport can also be expressed as a vertical heat flux.

Total vertical heat flux:

F_H = ρ₀ c_p wT - ρ₀ c_p K_T ∂T/∂z


where

ρ₀ : reference seawater density [kg m⁻³]
c_p : specific heat capacity of seawater [J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹]
w   : vertical velocity [m s⁻¹]
T   : temperature
K_T : vertical thermal diffusivity [m² s⁻¹]


The flux can be separated into:

Advective heat flux:

F_H,adv = ρ₀ c_p wT


Diffusive/turbulent heat flux:

F_H,diff = -ρ₀ c_p K_T ∂T/∂z


The negative sign indicates downgradient transport: turbulent diffusion tends
to transport heat from warmer regions toward colder regions.


5. Reynolds-averaged turbulent heat transport
---------------------------------------------

If temperature and vertical velocity are decomposed into mean and fluctuating
components,

T = T̄ + T'
w = w̄ + w'

then turbulent vertical temperature transport is represented by

w'T' averaged in time or ensemble:

overline(w'T')


Under a downgradient diffusion closure,

overline(w'T') = -K_T ∂T̄/∂z


and the corresponding turbulent vertical heat flux is

F_H,turb = ρ₀ c_p overline(w'T')
         = -ρ₀ c_p K_T ∂T̄/∂z


6. Physical interpretation
--------------------------

The vertical redistribution of ocean temperature is primarily controlled by
two distinct mechanisms:

1) Vertical advection:
   Heat is carried vertically by organized fluid motion through w.

2) Vertical turbulent mixing:
   Heat is redistributed across the vertical temperature gradient through K_T.

Thus, when examining how surface thermal signals are transmitted into the
subsurface ocean, the key quantities are:

- vertical velocity w
- vertical temperature gradient ∂T/∂z
- vertical diffusivity K_T
- turbulent heat flux overline(w'T')


7. Key equation for subsurface-temperature studies
--------------------------------------------------

For studies focused on surface-to-subsurface temperature transfer, a useful
form is:

∂T/∂t
=
- u ∂T/∂x
- v ∂T/∂y
- w ∂T/∂z
+ ∇_h · (K_h ∇_h T)
+ ∂/∂z (K_T ∂T/∂z)
+ Q_T


This form separates the processes responsible for temporal temperature change:

- horizontal advection
- vertical advection
- horizontal mixing
- vertical mixing
- external/internal heating or cooling

In particular, the terms

-w ∂T/∂z

and

∂/∂z (K_T ∂T/∂z)

describe the principal dynamical and mixing mechanisms that transfer
temperature vertically within the ocean.
